Critique is a second pass, not a first. Run the objective checks before the
matter-of-opinion ones, because the objective findings are not arguable and
fixing them usually resolves half the complaints.

**Mechanical tells, in rough order of how often they appear.** Spacing that
repeats one value everywhere instead of following a scale. Three or four font
weights doing the job of two. A gradient standing in for hierarchy. One border
radius and one shadow stamped on every element, which flattens everything to the
same importance. Everything centred. Emoji as section markers. Cards for things
that are not objects. A hero sized to the viewport rather than to its contents,
pushing the actual page below the fold.

**Then the judgement layer.** Does the eye land where the page wants it to, or
does everything compete? Is there one thing on the screen that is deliberately
the loudest? Does the type carry the personality, or is it doing nothing while
colour and decoration do the work? Would this look like anyone's page, or like
this subject's page?

Be specific about location and fix. "The spacing is inconsistent" is not usable.
"The hero uses 18px, 22px and 25px gaps where the rest of the page is on an
8px scale" is.

Separate what is wrong from what is merely not to your taste, and say which is
which. A design can be unlike what you would have made and still be correct;
saying so is more useful than quietly rewriting it toward the average.
