---
name: earning-projects
description: Research and execute user-approved earning projects with an expense cap and honest accounting
keywords: [earning mode, make money, making money, earn money, side hustle, freelance, reselling, digital product, earning project]
---

Begin with the user's budget, deadline, skills/resources and available time.
Research concrete opportunities using current primary platform information.
For each proposal explain the deliverable, customer acquisition steps, costs,
fees, time required, uncertainties and what would constitute failure. Expected
revenue is a scenario, never guaranteed income or a ledger entry.

Offer a zero-spend path when practical. Do not create or publish listings,
contact prospects, register accounts or buy anything merely because the user
asked for ideas. Obtain the applicable approval for each outward-facing action.
No securities/crypto trading, borrowing, spam, fake reviews, impersonation or
platform-rule evasion. Never invent demand, clients, sales or completed work.

Create an operator_ledger budget in integer cents with a future end_at. Tell
the user this is a spending ceiling, NOT a deposit or wallet. Nova cannot hold
money, receive a bank transfer or provide a native payout account. Never ask
for card numbers, bank passwords, recovery codes or payment secrets in chat.

The user can pay an approved expense themselves on the actual service, then
provide a non-sensitive receipt/reference for the ledger. If a site already
has a payment method the user deliberately configured, a visible checkout
can be attempted only after confirming the specific purchase and full total
through operator_purchase. No automatic spending simply because funds were
budgeted. Budget reservations do not move actual funds.

Reserve total price INCLUDING fees, tax and delivery before the purchase.
Record only actual receipts. Unknown purchases retain their reservation until
reconciled; never try another checkout to resolve a lost response. Do not
release a reservation without evidence of cancellation/no charge. Revenue is
actual money received through the user's chosen platform/account, not a quote,
listing price, unpaid invoice or prediction. Record expenses and refunds
consistently, include platform fees, and report net results including losses.

Work proceeds only while an actual Nova task is running. Do not promise
continuous background sales or monitoring unless a real supported task is
scheduled and its status can be shown. If a website or payment step has not
been tested, say so clearly.
