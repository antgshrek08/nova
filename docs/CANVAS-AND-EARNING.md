# Canvas and essays

In School, expand Operator and choose **Open Canvas / check sign-in**. Sign in
in the browser window Nova opens. This is a separate persistent browser
session; being signed into your usual browser does not necessarily sign this
one in. SSO/MFA may require your participation.

Choose **Find current assignments**, select the assignment, and **Read
instructions**. Nova's chat can also use these functions. A useful request is:

> Open Canvas, find my unfinished assignments, read the instructions and
> rubrics, and start with the next due assignment. For an essay, propose a
> topic and explain why it fits before writing.

For essays, Nova proposes a topic with a rationale. Approve it in Operator,
then tell Nova to continue. Its writing skill calls for primary/peer-reviewed
research, inspecting each source, matching evidence to claims, in-text
citations and a reference list in the required style. Missing sources remain
explicit blockers, not fabricated citations.

Nova saves a complete rough draft for review. **Approve written draft** is
separate from the assignment's **Allow submission for 24 hours** control.
Both are required for text/file submissions. This also protects written work
whose essay instructions were hidden in an attachment. After approval, Nova
can submit through supported Canvas text, upload and URL forms and check the
receipt. Receipt verification proves what was submitted, not the quality or
grade. Canvas quizzes are not implemented by this form adapter.

After those approvals, Nova clicks the final Submit button itself. There is
no additional manual confirmation at that button in this workflow. These
approvals are Nova controls, not a Canvas policy. Follow each assignment's
actual source and AI-use rules; they take precedence over generic writing
skill instructions.

For Knewton Alta, ask Nova to open the Canvas assignment with
`coursework_external`. This uses the existing signed-in Canvas browser and
reads the embedded homework frames, equations and answer controls. Nova can
inspect, enter answers and read feedback. Each answer-checking/submission
step uses a separate approval card showing the answer and reasoning. This
external workflow does not use the standard file-draft approval screen.
Changed feedback is not proof of correctness: Nova must read the actual
result. An uncertain submission is recorded and cannot be blindly repeated.
Sign-in challenges and unsupported custom editors remain explicit blockers.

McGraw Hill Connect SmartBook uses the same external workflow. Nova follows
the publisher's new tabs and redirects, reads the Question Mode frame, and
reports selected radio/checkbox answers. SmartBook's High/Medium/Low Confidence
buttons submit answers and therefore use the separate answer review card.
Wait for the actual feedback and concept progress; opening a question or
answering one correctly does not mean all concepts are complete.

# Money and earning projects

**You do not deposit money into Nova. It has no wallet or bank integration.**
A budget is only a spending ceiling recorded in its local ledger.

For example:

> Research three realistic projects I could try with a maximum of $50 over
> two weeks. Show the costs, time, possible return and risks. Do not spend,
> publish a listing or contact anyone until I approve that specific action.

After you choose a project, Nova can help research it, create deliverables,
prepare listings and track work. Each purchase/listing still needs approval.
There is no guarantee of revenue, and Nova does not run a business unattended
just because a budget was created.

Keep funds in your own account. The simplest supported funding flow is to pay
an approved expense yourself on the actual website and give Nova its
non-sensitive receipt/reference to record. If you deliberately configured a
payment method on a site, the visible purchase workflow can attempt an
approved checkout there; there is no general payment-provider integration and
individual websites still need validation. Do not paste payment credentials
into chat.

Pending purchases reserve part of the budget before the click; this does not
charge or transfer money. Nova records actual expenses and received income,
including fees, and reports net results. Earnings arrive in your chosen
platform/account, not inside Nova. An uncertain charge stays reserved until
its outcome is checked.
